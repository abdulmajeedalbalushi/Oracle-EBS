Make the "DEPT" master block and the "EMP" detail block.

1. Visit Oracle Forms Builder.
2. Make a CANVAS named CAN_DEPT_EMP.
3. Make a data block named DEPT and store it in CAN_DEPT_EMP.
4. Make a data block named EMP and place it in CAN_DEPT_EMP.
5. You'll be asked to establish a connection.
6. Click Create Relationship after selecting Remove Auto Join.
            Block Master: DEPT
            Master Item: DEPTNO
            Item of Detail: DEPTNO
            Join Shown: EMP.DEPTNO = DEPT.DEPTNO
7. By default, three programs and three triggers will be written.
8. To generate a ABDULMAJEEDMASTERDETAIL.fmb, SAVE
9. Make a ABDULMAJEEDMASTERDETAIL.fmx file.
